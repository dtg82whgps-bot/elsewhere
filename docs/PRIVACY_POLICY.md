# Elsewhere Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This is the policy to publish at the URL set in `mobile/app.json` (`extra.privacyPolicyUrl`). The same text is shown in the app under **Privacy & terms**. Keep both in step.

## What Elsewhere does

Elsewhere helps you explore the realistic consequences of a decision. You describe a decision, optionally add details about yourself, and choose how to see it: three possible futures, or five pros and five cons. The analysis draws on current, cited sources.

## Information you provide

- **Decision text**: the decision you type in.
- **Profile details (all optional)**: age, location, savings, income, education, employment, career goals, priorities, and any notes you choose to add.

Your profile is stored **only on your device**. It is not sent anywhere until you run an analysis. You can delete it at any time from the About you screen.

## What is sent to our analysis service

When you run an analysis, the decision and any profile details you have entered are sent to Elsewhere's analysis service over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The service asks an artificial intelligence model, Anthropic's Claude, to write search queries and then to write your analysis. Claude also runs web searches to find information relevant to your decision.

- We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or combine it with other data about you.
- The analysis service does not store your decision, profile, or analysis after the response is returned.
- The service keeps your IP address in memory for up to one hour, only to limit how many requests one device can make. It is not written to a database.
- Error messages may be written to server logs. They are written without your personal details.

## Service providers

- **Anthropic** processes the content of your analysis requests to provide the AI model and web search. Anthropic's handling of that data is governed by its own terms and data policies for its API.
- **Hosting provider** for the analysis service: [to be named by the operator before release].

## Your choices

- You are asked for permission before your first analysis. You can withdraw it by deleting your data in the About you screen.
- Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device.

## Children

Elsewhere is not intended for children under 18 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes to this policy

If we change how information is used, we will update this policy and, where the change is material, ask for your permission again before the next analysis.
